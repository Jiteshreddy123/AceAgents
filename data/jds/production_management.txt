Job Title: Technical Product Manager (TPM)
Location: Austin, TX (On-site)
Company: NextGen Robotics

Position Overview:
NextGen Robotics is building the future of automated warehouse logistics. We are searching for a Technical Product Manager to lead our Core Platform team. You will bridge the gap between complex engineering realities and business strategic goals.

What You Will Do:
- Own the product roadmap, lifecycle, and backlog prioritization for our internal APIs and developer platforms.
- Write detailed PRDs (Product Requirement Documents) and technical user stories.
- Act as the primary interface between hardware engineering, software engineering, and customer success teams.

What We Are Looking For:
- At least 4 years of experience as a Product Manager, Technical Program Manager, or Scrum Master in an agile environment.
- Prior background as a software engineer or systems architect is highly desirable.

Required Competencies & Tools:
- Agile Methodologies: Scrum, Kanban
- Project Management Tools: Jira, Confluence
- Technical Frameworks: RESTful APIs, Microservices Architecture, System Design basics
- Product Analytics: Mixpanel, Google Analytics
